About Me

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), advised by Prof. Jian Wang. I am with the Microprocessor Research Center and the State Key Lab of Processors.

Research Interests

My interests lie in computer architecture for heterogeneous computing, with a current focus on chiplet die-to-die (D2D) interconnects.

  • Chiplet interconnects: link architecture, flow control, and reliability.
  • Workload-driven performance modeling: traffic characterization and bottleneck analysis.
  • Heterogeneous memory systems: memory fabrics and data movement.

My background in analog circuits and high-speed wired communication informs how I think about link constraints and system-level performance.
