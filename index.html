About Me

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), advised by Prof. Jian Wang. I am with the Microprocessor Research Center and the State Key Lab of Processors.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in computer architecture for AI systems, particularly chiplet interconnects and memory fabrics for AI scale-up systems. I am currently exploring how die-to-die link mechanisms, physical constraints, and workload characteristics jointly shape system-level performance.

  • Chiplet and die-to-die interconnect architectures for AI scale-up systems
  • Mechanism-level performance modeling, bottleneck analysis, and workload-driven optimization
  • Data movement and memory systems for heterogeneous AI computing

My background in analog circuits and high-speed wired communication informs my cross-layer approach to interconnect architecture, from physical link constraints to workload-visible behavior.
